### Skills to Know About
- /init — scans the repo and writes a CLAUDE.md so future sessions know the architecture. Run it once per repo, or after a big structural change.
- /update-config — edits `.claude/settings.json` / `settings.local.json`: permission allow/deny rules, hooks, env. Use it to let run/test commands through without prompts and to deny risky ones (reading `.env`, destructive commands).
- /compact — summarizes a long conversation so you can keep working past the context limit. Reach for it when a session gets long mid-task.
